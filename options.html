🔐 AI Settings

Configure your AI provider for component generation.

Your API key is stored locally in your browser and never sent anywhere except directly to the AI provider.
Leave blank to use the default for your provider.
⚠️ Security Note: This extension stores your API key in chrome.storage.local (not synced to your Google account). Your key is sent directly to the AI provider's API from your browser. As with any client-side application, your API key is not fully secret from anyone with local access to your machine.

💾 Backup & Restore

Export your environments to a file, or import from one.

Importing adds environments from the file to your existing list — it never removes or overwrites what you already have.
